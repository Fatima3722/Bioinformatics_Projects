# Fatima Rahman — research and computational portfolio

My background is in biochemistry, and I am developing my research at the interface of microbiology, bioinformatics and quantitative biology. I am particularly interested in diet–microbiome interactions, undernutrition, antimicrobial resistance and how microbial interactions contribute to host development.

## Research direction

My ongoing research involves microbiome and resistome analysis in the context of nutrition. I am interested in combining careful data analysis with mechanistic questions about microbial communities and host–microbe interactions.

The repositories below contain public computational examples. They are separate from ongoing research analyses and should not be interpreted as published findings from those projects.
